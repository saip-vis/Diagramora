About Voice to Flowchart

Diagramming should begin with understanding, not drawing tools.

Voice to Flowchart is an early-stage project exploring a simpler way to turn spoken explanations into structured, editable workflows. The goal is to let people focus on the process they understand while the software handles nodes, branches, and layout.

Flowcharts are the starting point. Over time, the same approach can support other structured visuals such as decision trees, system diagrams, UML, and organization charts.

Natural firstExplain ideas in your own words instead of learning a drawing interface.
Editable alwaysAI creates the first draft; people stay in control of the final result.
Built in the openPrivate testing and real feedback guide what becomes part of the product.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the gestures?
Speak naturally while you record. Gestures are optional and help confirm or refine what you say. Here's what each one does:
👍 Thumbs Up
Confirms the step or connection you just mentioned is correct.
☝️ Pointing
Emphasizes or indicates the specific step you're talking about.
✊ Closed Fist
Undoes or removes the most recently mentioned step.
👉 Swipe Right
"After this" links the subject before and after the swipe in sequence.
👈 Swipe Left
Similar to swipe right, but can indicate reversing or going back a step.
🙌 Two-Hand Branch
Both palms open, one on each side of frame, splits the current subject into two separate paths.
📦 Box Motion
Spread two fingers apart, then bring them back together to mark the current idea as its own separate node.
Do I need to record on a specific browser?
Yes. Live transcription uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which currently works reliably in Chrome. Other browsers may not support it.
Is my audio or video saved anywhere?
Camera frames are processed in your browser for hand-landmark detection and are not intentionally stored by Voice to Flowchart. Speech recognition may be handled by your browser provider; the resulting transcript is sent to the application to build the diagram. See Privacy for more detail.
Why does the flowchart sometimes look messy while I'm still talking?
The live view prioritizes speed, so it builds the graph in real time as you speak. This can occasionally produce a rough or duplicate node. Once you click Stop, a final cleanup pass checks everything against your full explanation before the downloadable version is created.
Can I edit the flowchart after it's generated?
Yes. Open the editor to change node text and styling, adjust connections, add or remove steps, undo changes, or describe an edit in natural language.
What is included in the free trial?
Free accounts include three completed AI-generated flowcharts and ten AI-assisted edits. Manual edits, saving, reopening, and exporting existing designs do not use trial credits.
What do the thick and thin lines mean?
Thick lines mark the main, expected path through your process. Thin lines mark secondary paths such as error handling, rejections, or side branches.

Privacy Policy

Temporary private-testing policy · Effective August 1, 2026

What this app collects

Flowchart App processes the account information you provide, your transcribed process descriptions, detected gesture names and timestamps, generated flowchart data, designs you choose to save, and feedback you choose to submit.

Microphone and camera use

The current browser experience uses your microphone for speech recognition and your camera for live hand-landmark detection. Camera frames are processed in the browser and are not intentionally stored by Flowchart App. Speech recognition may be processed by your browser or browser provider. The resulting transcript, not your live camera feed, is sent to the application to generate a flowchart.

AI and service providers

Transcript text, gesture events, and relevant flowchart data may be sent to OpenAI to generate, repair, title, or edit diagrams. Supabase is used for account authentication and for storing profiles, saved designs, transcripts, version snapshots, trial usage, and technical AI usage metadata such as model names and token counts.

How information is used

  • To create, edit, export, save, and reopen your diagrams.
  • To authenticate your account and keep designs associated with you.
  • To diagnose errors and improve the private testing experience.

Your choices

You can use Start Over to clear the current browser workspace, delete saved designs from My Designs, and stop camera or microphone access through your browser settings. This test build limits each account to three saved designs.

Private-testing notice

This application is an early private test and should not be used for confidential, regulated, medical, financial, legal, or highly sensitive information. This temporary policy will be revised before a broader public launch.
